                                       Conclusion: In our sample of adults with DS and MCI-DS, 31.6% did not progress to

                                    dementia despite having high levels of amyloid PET. Those whose decline plateaued

                                  had more medical conditions and life stressors. Those with temporary decline had more

                                          psychiatric problems. Primary care physicians need to understand which reversible or

                                         treatable factors are most likely to lead to a misdiagnosis of prodromal AD and address

                                them accordingly.
